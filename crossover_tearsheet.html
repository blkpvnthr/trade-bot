Read

The edge is survivorship, not strategy. Headline gates-off beats SPY (4.44y +77% vs +65%; 1y +69% vs +24%), but dropping just 2 names flips the 4.44y result below SPY risk-adjusted (Sharpe 0.77→0.62), and dropping 6 collapses it (0.40). The 44-name list was curated today, so a back-replay bakes in the winners — ~6 biotech names (ERAS/AXSM/CELC/NUVL/ORKA/SYRE) carry ~70% of the gain.

In the 1-year up-only tape, most gates never bind. Regime (SPY never dipped below its 200-DMA), name-cap, max-tranches and cash-buffer are non-binding — only the 80% exposure cap constrains, costing ~11 pts of return for almost no Sharpe gain. The two selection proxies hurt (trend/dip filters cut return ~18–21 pts) — any selectivity that delays buying the curated winners is penalised in this window.

What's actually durable: the regime gate + caps are insurance — free in this melt-up, but in the 4.44y window (incl. 2022) the regime gate cut drawdown −35%→−25%. Gates-off isn't reckless (Sharpe ≥ SPY, DD controlled), but it shows no forward edge — it just dilutes into the basket and rides whatever's hot. Consistent with the rest of the system: only the insider copier has a validated edge.
