<objective>
## Goal
Add Alpaca data provenance diagnostics to forecast_engine.py and rl_intraday_structure_trainer.py so both files log their CSV source at load time and fail clearly if called with stale pre-migration data.

## Purpose
forecast_engine.py and rl_intraday_structure_trainer.py are CSV consumers — they don't fetch market data directly. Their data provenance is inherited entirely from build_intraday_training_dataset.py, which was updated this session to use ALPACA_REGULAR_FEED. Adding explicit [data-source] log lines at CSV load time makes the feed provenance chain visible in logs and surfaceable in diagnostics, consistent with the pattern already established in premarket_trade_planner.py.

## Output
- forecast_engine.py: `[data-source] training_csv=alpaca_derived` logged in `_load_intraday()` on successful load
- rl_intraday_structure_trainer.py: `[data-source] training_csv=alpaca_derived` logged in `load_bars()` on successful load
- Both files: `python -m py_compile` passes clean
</objective>
